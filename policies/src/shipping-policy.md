# Shipping Policy

Source for /policies/shipping-policy. Run python policies/build.py after editing.

---

## Cost

Shipping is free on every order, to every country we ship to. There are no thresholds and no surcharges.

## Delivery time

Most orders arrive within 1 to 2 weeks of the day you order, processing included. Deliveries to remote areas and some countries outside North America and Europe can take a little longer.

## Tracking

You will receive a tracking link by email when your order dispatches. Tracking can take 2 to 5 business days to begin updating after dispatch. This is normal and does not mean the parcel is lost.

## Holiday delivery

For delivery before a specific date, please order with at least three weeks of margin. We cannot guarantee delivery dates, since transit is handled by carriers and customs authorities outside our control.

## Customs and import duties

Orders shipped outside our dispatch country may be subject to import duties or taxes levied by the destination country. These are set by your government, are not collected by us, and are the responsibility of the recipient. Most orders at this value are not charged duty, but we cannot predict or control this.

## Incorrect addresses

Please check your shipping address carefully at checkout. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incorrect or incomplete, we will refund you or reship at your choice. Contact us within 24 hours of ordering if you need to correct an address.

## Gift orders

Every order ships gift-ready. No invoice, price tag, or packing slip showing any amount is included in the package. Your receipt is emailed to you only.
