## Who we are

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME], [REGISTERED ADDRESS], operating givedearly.com. For privacy questions, email hello@givedearly.com.

## What we collect

Information you give us: name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number if you provide one, and order contents. Payment card details go directly to our payment processor and we never see or store the full number.

Information collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages viewed, referring site, and similar analytics data, gathered through cookies and similar technologies.

Marketing information: if you subscribe, your email address and your engagement with our emails.

## Why we use it

To process and deliver your order; to communicate with you about it; to handle returns and support; to prevent fraud; to understand how the site is used so we can improve it; and, where you have consented or where we have a legitimate interest permitted by law, to show you relevant advertising and send marketing emails.

## Who we share it with

We share only what is necessary, with: Shopify (our store and checkout platform); our payment processors; our fulfilment partners and shipping carriers, who receive your name and delivery address; our email provider; and analytics and advertising providers including Meta Platforms. We do not sell your personal information.

Some of these providers are located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

## Advertising

We advertise on Meta platforms and use the Meta pixel to measure results and show ads to people likely to be interested. You can control this through your Meta ad preferences and through our cookie banner.

## How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as required for tax and accounting purposes, generally six to seven years. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept for up to 26 months.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising these rights. To make a request, email hello@givedearly.com. We respond within one month. If you are in the UK or EU you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children

Our store is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## Security

Our site runs over HTTPS and payments are handled by PCI-compliant processors. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
