## Returns

You have 90 days from the delivery date to return any item for a full refund. We do not ask why.

To start a return, email thedearlyco@gmail.com with your order number. We will send return instructions. You do not need to provide photographs, fill in a form, or explain your reason.

Items should be returned in any condition. We do not require original packaging.

## Refunds

Once your return is on its way back to us, we issue your refund to the original payment method. Refunds typically appear within 5 to 10 business days depending on your bank. We refund the full purchase price. Original shipping was free, so there is nothing to deduct.

## Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, email thedearlyco@gmail.com with a photograph. We will send a replacement at no cost and you do not need to return the original item.

## Items lost in transit

If tracking shows no movement for 14 consecutive days, or your order has not arrived within 30 days of dispatch, contact us and we will either replace it or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

## Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before dispatch. Email us as soon as possible. Once an order has shipped it falls under the return policy above, which still gives you 90 days.

## Contact

Email thedearlyco@gmail.com and we’ll answer within 24 hours.
