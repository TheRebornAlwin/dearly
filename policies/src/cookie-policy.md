# Cookie Policy

Shopify has no Cookie policy field under Settings, Policies. Two options:
1. Create a page at Online Store, Pages, titled "Cookie Policy" with the handle `cookie-policy` and paste this in. The footer links to `/pages/cookie-policy` by default.
2. Or append this text to the end of the Privacy policy, and change the footer's Cookie Policy link to `/policies/privacy-policy`.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as pixels and local storage, and we refer to all of them as cookies here.

## The categories we use

Strictly necessary. Required for the site to work: keeping your cart, remembering you through checkout, security, and load balancing. These cannot be switched off and are set by Shopify.

Analytics. Help us understand which pages people visit and where they leave, so we can improve the store. Set by Shopify analytics and, if enabled, Google Analytics.

Marketing. Let us measure our advertising and show relevant ads. This includes the Meta pixel, which records actions such as viewing a product or completing a purchase and reports them to Meta.

## Your choices

When you first visit, our banner lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. Declining is a one-click action, not a menu you have to dig through, and the site works fully either way. You can change your choice at any time through the cookie preferences link in our footer.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though strictly necessary cookies are required for checkout to function.

## Third parties

Cookies set by Shopify, Meta, and our analytics providers are governed by their own privacy policies, which you can find on their websites.
